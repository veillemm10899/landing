02 — The VEILLEMM Standard

Built like a product,
lived like a home.

VEILLEMM was founded by Cyrus Vergara — a developer who treats every residence the way he ships software: research first, prototype, refine, then hand over something he would gladly live in himself.

That means no inherited floor plans and no shortcuts. Every home we touch starts with how you move through a day — morning light, cooking, hosting, quiet corners — and is then engineered to serve that rhythm. The result is a small portfolio of homes that feel less like inventory and more like they were waiting for the right owner.

03 — How It Works

Four steps from
“someday” to “home.”

  1. 01

    Discover

    A private session on your budget, your routines, and the address you keep picturing. We map a shortlist of sites and homes that actually fit.

  2. 02

    Design

    Floor plans re-worked around light and movement, materials chosen for how they age, and a full digital twin so you walk the home before it exists.

  3. 03

    Build

    A single general contractor with weekly photo reports and a fixed completion date. No surprise line items, ever.

  4. 04

    Handover

    Styled, documented, and delivered with every warranty in hand. You move in — we stay one call away.
