DEBRIEFING // Q&A

QUESTIONS THEY'D RATHER YOU NOT ASK

Is AI actually dangerous — or is this fear-mongering?+

Danger doesn't require a conscious villain. A tool that can fabricate reality, automate decisions, and scale errors infinitely is dangerous by definition. The incidents in this dossier already happened — that's not fear, that's history.

Will AI take my job?+

It is already taking tasks. The jobs that survive are the ones that require judgment, accountability, and taste — the things a machine can imitate but never own. Those are the skills worth building.

How do I know if a video or voice is real?+

Assume nothing. Check the source channel, look for micro-artifacts (skin texture, teeth, blinking), and verify through an independent channel. If it's asking for money, it's fake until proven otherwise.

Can AI be regulated in time?+

Regulation moves at the speed of committees; models improve at the speed of compute. The gap between them is where the damage happens. Personal vigilance is the only regulation that ships today.

Why hire a human instead of an AI?+

Because a human can be held responsible. When software runs your business, you want someone who can explain it, defend it, and answer the phone when it fails. That person is a developer — not a prompt.
