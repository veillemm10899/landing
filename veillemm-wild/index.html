01 The manifesto

A developer's way of
giving back to the land.

Reforestation is full of promises and short on proof. Plots that never get planted, trees that never get checked, numbers that never get published. Cyrus — a developer who spent a decade shipping software — found that unacceptable.

So VEILLEMM was built like a codebase. Every plot is drone-mapped before a single seed. Every sapling is logged with an ID and a location. Every claim about survival is audited, quarterly, and published openly. Nature doesn't need our noise. It needs our follow-through.

02 Methods

No vibes.
Only follow-through.

  1. 01

    Ground-truth mapping

    Drone surveys and soil reads before a single seed touches the ground. We know the land before we work it.

    →
  2. 02

    Native-only planting

    Species from local seed banks, matched to microclimate. Never exotic. Never a monoculture.

    →
  3. 03

    Survivorship tracking

    Every tree gets an ID. Audited quarterly, published openly. If it dies, we replant it — and say so.

    →
  4. 04

    Community partnership

    Local caretakers are paid and trained. The land stays guarded — and the knowledge stays local — for a decade.

03 Process

From satellite
to sapling.

  1. 01

    Survey

    Satellite + drone + soil sampling. A digital twin of the plot before anything is planted.

  2. 02

    Seed

    Seedlings raised in local nurseries from provenances native to the exact watershed.

  3. 03

    Plant

    Community crews plant in dense, layered clusters. Every sapling logged with coordinates and ID.

  4. 04

    Verify

    Quarterly drone flights and ground audits. Results published — survival, growth, carbon, honestly.

07 FAQ

Plain answers,
no greenwash.

How do you choose planting sites?

Degraded or deforested land with a willing local partner. We survey with drones and soil tests, confirm native seed availability, then commit to a 10-year care plan — not a one-time photo op.

Can I sponsor a tree or a plot?

Yes. Individuals can sponsor trees; companies can adopt whole plots for carbon offsets. Every sponsored tree gets an ID and appears in the public tracking dashboard.

How is survival actually verified?

Quarterly drone flights over every plot, plus ground audits by independent local crews. We publish survival, growth, and replant numbers openly — including the failures.

Do you work outside the Philippines?

Current plots are in the Philippines. The methods and the dashboard are built to travel — if your region has degraded land and a local partner, we're open to conversations.

Can I volunteer?

Yes — planting days are announced per plot, and we always need hands, nurseries, and tech volunteers. Send a message through the form and tell us what you're good at.
