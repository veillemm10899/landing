01  /  the story

Why I love
the rain

Some people see rain as a cancelled plan. I see it as permission — permission to slow down, to stay in, to think.

On days like these the world lowers its volume. The streets go dark and reflective, the house dims into a warm cocoon, and every task that felt loud and urgent suddenly becomes small and manageable. The rain doesn't interrupt my work — it frames it.

That's the feeling I chase in everything I build: interfaces that let people breathe, dark quiet spaces where the important things glow, and calm that never feels empty. The weather taught me that less can hold more.
