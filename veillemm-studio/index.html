01 — The Manifesto

One builder.
Total accountability.

Agencies hand your project down a chain — strategist to designer to developer to project manager — and somehow no one is ever responsible for the result.

VEILLEMM inverts that. One person sketches it, writes it, ships it, and stays on the line when something breaks. You're not buying a team of seven. You're buying one developer who behaves like seven — minus the meetings.

“You don't need more meetings.
You need fewer hand-offs.

  1. P.01

    Built by one

    Every line of code is written by the same hands that sketched the first wireframe. No translation loss between design and development — only intent, preserved.

  2. P.02

    Performance is a feature

    A beautiful page that takes six seconds to load isn't beautiful. VEILLEMM ships fast, measured, and ranked — with performance budgets treated like design constraints.

  3. P.03

    Design with conviction

    Trends are borrowed; direction is earned. Every typeface, grid, and pixel on this page answers to a reason — and so will yours.

04 — Process

A sprint,
not a saga.

No fourteen-phase waterfall, no 60-page proposals written to confuse you. Four steps, visible progress, and a working product at the end of each one.

  1. 01

    Align

    One call. Goal, audience, constraints, budget. You leave with a plan and a price — in writing, before a single line of code.

  2. 02

    Design

    Direction and working prototypes in days, not months. Decisions, not decks — so you feel the product before it exists.

  3. 03

    Build

    Production code with weekly demos and zero surprises. You watch the product take shape instead of waiting for a big reveal.

  4. 04

    Ship & support

    Deployed, measured, documented — then a support window while you take the keys. Clean code you actually own, anywhere you want it.

06 — FAQ

Answers
before you ask.

The questions every founder asks before the first call — answered straight, the way you'll be answered for the rest of the engagement.

How much does a project cost?+

Engagements begin with a fixed, itemized proposal after the first call — no hourly mystery bills. Most web products land somewhere between a focused sprint and a full build; you'll know the exact number before any code is written.

How fast can you ship?+

A focused MVP typically ships in 14–30 days, depending on scope. You get a working product and weekly demos along the way — never a reveal at the end.

Do you work with existing codebases?+

Yes — rescue and rebuild work is a specialty. Messy repositories, inherited deadlines, and half-finished launches are routine, and often the most valuable work I do.

How does communication work?+

Direct, written, and on a schedule. You talk to Cyrus — the person building your product — never to a queue or an account manager.

Can you maintain the product after launch?+

Every build includes a support window, and longer retainers are available. You'll also own clean, documented code you can take to any team you like.
