03 // THE OPERATION

Your 6-step operation order

A week-by-week plan. 10–15 hours a week gets you job-ready in 6–12 months.

  1. 01

    Learn the fundamentals

    Networking, Linux, Windows internals, OSI model. 6–8 weeks. Free: TryHackMe, Professor Messer, overthewire.

  2. 02

    Build a home lab

    VirtualBox + Kali + a practice target (Metasploitable, DVWA). Break things on purpose — it's free reps.

  3. 03

    Earn your first cert

    Security+ first — it's the HR filter. Then specialize: CEH, SSCP, or AWS Security depending on your path.

  4. 04

    Get public reps

    Write up 10 labs on a blog or GitHub. Do bug-bounty lite on HackTheBox. Employers read your trail.

  5. 05

    Apply with a war chest

    Resume, portfolio, LinkedIn optimized. Target junior SOC, NOC, or IT-support-with-security roles first.

  6. 06

    Level up on the job

    First 12 months: keep a lab journal, add one cert, specialize. That's when salaries jump 60–100%.

07 // FIELD INTEL

Questions recruits ask

Do I really need a degree?

No. The 4.8M-person gap means skills win. A Security+ cert plus a documented home lab consistently beats an unrelated degree in shortlists.

How long until my first job?

At 10–15 focused hours a week: 6 months to a junior SOC or IT security support role, 12 months if you're starting from zero tech background.

Can I work remotely from the Philippines?

Yes. PH-based SOCs and BPO security teams hire locally at ₱50K+ entry, and remote US roles pay at global rates if you have certs + English + portfolio.

Which cert first, Security+ or CEH?

Security+ first — it's the common HR filter and cheaper. CEH is a great second rung if you want the offensive path.
