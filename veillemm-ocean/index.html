01  /  the story

Why the ocean
pulls me under

The ocean doesn't ask permission. It pulls, it shifts, it swallows the horizon whole — and in that surrender, I find the purest focus.

Beneath the surface the world goes quiet. The noise of daily life fades, replaced by the hum of currents and the slow dance of light filtering through salt water. Down there, every detail matters — a barnacle, a ripple, a bioluminescent spark. The ocean doesn't tolerate noise, and that's exactly why I build like it: with intention, with depth, and with relentless attention to the details that make everything breathe.

That's the feeling I chase in everything I build: interfaces as vast as the open sea, experiences as immersive as diving into the blue, and work that carries the weight and wonder of the deep.
