Our story — 01

01

A developer's obsession,
poured into a cup.

VEILLEMM began in Cyrus Vergara's kitchen — one scale, one kettle, and a stubborn pursuit of the perfect pour. Between commits, between sprints, the ritual stayed.

What started as a developer's morning habit became a roastery obsessed with precision: every bean sourced from farmers we've met in person, every batch logged like a code commit, every cup measured to the gram. We treat coffee the way we treat software — we iterate until it's right.

Good to know — 06

Questions,
answered plainly.

Do you ship beans nationwide?

Yes. Beans ship within 24 hours of roasting in sealed, one-way valve bags. Free shipping on orders over ₱1,200 — roast date printed right on the bag.

Where is the café located?

We run as a slow-bar roastery with a rotating guest table program. The exact location and calendar are shared when you book — message us and we'll send the details.

Do you have non-dairy options?

Oat, soy, and lactose-free are always available at no extra charge. The Matcha Cloud and Slow Bar are built around oat by default.

Can I work or study here?

Quiet hours run 9–11 AM — plenty of power outlets, fast wifi, and nobody rushing your table. It's the corner we designed for exactly that.

Can you brew for my event?

Yes — we bring the slow bar to private events, launches, and team offsites. Send the date and headcount through the form and we'll build a menu around it.
