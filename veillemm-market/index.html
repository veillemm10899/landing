Why VEILLEMM

Built like software,
served like family.

This market was designed by Cyrus Vergara — a developer who noticed grocery shopping was skipping on trust. Long lines, wilted greens, mystery sourcing. So he rebuilt the whole idea around one rule: honest freshness at honest prices.

Every aisle, every daily deal, every checkout experience is intentionally engineered — the same attention to user experience you'd expect from great software, but for your pantry.
