/// LEVEL 00 — MANIFESTO

The web is a game console nobody fully uses.

Every browser is a machine that can run games. Almost no one builds for it properly. I do.

This started when a kid cracked the school computer's lock screen so the whole class could play Snake at 1:00 AM during a LAN event. Not to break rules — to prove a machine can be a playground. Years later, that obsession became VEILLEMM.

VEILLEMM is a one-person studio. I write the design, the code, the physics, the sound feel, the juice. When a game leaves my hands it is tested, optimized, and shipped like software — not like a portfolio toy. It runs at 60fps on a phone from three years ago and on a 4K monitor.

You can play three of those machines on this very page. If they feel good, imagine what I could build for your players, your brand, or your own game project.

/// LEVEL 05 — FAQ

Press start for answers

Can you build a game from my own idea?+

Yes. Send the concept — even half a page of notes. I'll shape it into a design doc, then build a playable graybox before we spend on polish.

What do browser games cost?+

Depends on scope: a simple arcade game starts around a fixed project price; brand interactives and longer games are quoted after the design doc. No hourly billing surprises.

How long does a game take?+

One polished arcade game typically ships in 2–4 weeks. Prototypes can be playable in days. I set explicit milestone dates and hit them.

Will it run on old phones?+

That's the core rule at VEILLEMM. Everything I build targets 60fps on mid-range devices from three years ago, then scales up to 4K desktops.

Do you also do full websites and apps?+

Yes — I'm a full-stack developer. Games are the specialty, but I ship complete digital products. See the portfolio for the range.
