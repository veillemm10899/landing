01 — Story

The web is not
a document.

04 — Process

Four moves, one night.

  1. 01

    Listen

    A call. Goals, audience, constraints, budget. You leave with a direction and a number — in writing.

  2. 02

    Score

    Art direction and a working prototype you can feel before a line of production code exists.

  3. 03

    Rehearse

    Weekly builds and live demos with honest notes. No black boxes, no surprise reveals.

  4. 04

    Perform

    Deploy, measure, hand over clean code you own — then support while you take the stage.
